# Créditos hipotecarios

Implemented locally on 2026-10-21. No release, deployment or deployed-state verification is implied.

## Owner workflow

1. Create **Crédito hipotecario** in Créditos, product settings, or inline from a bank/cash payment. Start incomplete if necessary, then confirm COP/UVR denomination, principal and closing date, contract rate/convention, method, remaining installments, next due date, insurance/fees and any subsidy dates. Use native decimal values with a decimal point for capital/UVR/rates; COP payment fields accept Colombian formatting.
2. Upload the latest Bancolombia mortgage XLSX, current annual projection and as much available history as useful. Multiple files prepare sequentially; confirm each preview. Monthly coverage does not prove a complete loan history. Files do not create withdrawals or change debt automatically.
3. In a new or edited bank/cash movement choose **Categoría de gasto → Créditos → Vivienda → Crédito destino**, then its actual bank application: ordinary installment, principal reducing term, principal reducing installment, or installment advance. The direct **Pago de crédito hipotecario** type remains available. Category and subcategory stay visible. Keep unknown distributions pending. A remembered merchant identifies the loan without reusing the previous purpose or component proportions. An optional historical preview selects up to 20 compatible movements of the original merchant per save, excluding other loans and non-bank/cash sources. New links stay unconfirmed; existing payment applications, allocations, documents and all captured evidence remain intact.
4. In Documents select same-date historical component rows and confirm the matching pre-upload movement. Positive component totals must equal that movement. Zero-cash benefits, forecast rows and subsidy tables stay evidence only. A disclosed rounding difference up to COP 1.00 may be confirmed explicitly against interest; original values remain visible. Several plausible movements require an owner choice.
5. **Ajustar saldo según extracto** accepts a dated manual balance or confirmed document evidence, reason and optional updated due data. Its principal is the closing balance: all payments on that date and earlier are already included. UVR requires units plus a separately dated COP conversion. Adjusting debt changes neither cash nor spending. History is reversible; out-of-order documents do not supersede a later effective closing date.
6. **Simulador** compares an unchanged schedule, a principal abono reducing term and one reducing installment. It supports a lump sum plus monthly extras with explicit dates and budget. Lower installments conserve cash by default; keeping the total monthly budget is an advanced choice. Save assumptions or export the complete schedule CSV. A simulation never registers a real payment.

Ordinary/advance payments identify the due date they settle. This suppresses the next mortgage obligation in available cash; an existing explicitly loan-linked bill is not reserved again. Extraordinary principal does not cancel a scheduled installment automatically. The product rail shows **Disponible total**, using the same global value as Resumen. Mortgage principal stays visible in Créditos and is never deducted in full from available cash. No household net-worth aggregate appears in the rail.

## Calculation and evidence limits

- Equivalent monthly constant-payment COP and constant-payment UVR only. Other methods/day counts are tracking-only. Decimal arithmetic uses 40 significant digits with half-up rounding; COP persists in centavos, schedule UVR rounds to four decimals. A final payment absorbs residual principal without a negative balance.
- Gross contracted interest and subsidy are converted independently to period rates. A coverage end date is required. The model recalculates after a coverage change; it does not establish eligibility or infer an end date from origination. Insurance and fees remain fixed confirmed assumptions unless the owner updates them.
- Between-due-date abonos use a disclosed monthly approximation. This is not an exact lender payoff quote or reproduction of daily accrual/early-payment discounts. Imported adjustments and original bank schedules remain separate evidence. A different bank application is corrected by reconciliation or a dated debt snapshot.
- UVR future COP values use an editable annual inflation assumption compounded over elapsed days/365, shared across strategies. Historical official quotes come from the public Suameca series 850, with source and date. A failed refresh exposes stale dated values or unavailability; no estimated UVR replaces an official quote. A quote fetch does not silently revalue confirmed evidence.
- The liquidity message compares the extra payment with current available cash after reserve and commitments. It is not a future household-income forecast or a bank affordability decision.

## Local validation

Synthetic tests cover accounting, source restrictions, missing allocations, reversals, closing snapshots, out-of-order documents, UVR conversion, budgets, zero interest, payoff, subsidy expiry, stale scenarios, source ownership, pre-upload comparison boundaries, repeated rows, and stale official-data cache. Browser tests cover creation, payment editing/reporting, imports, adjustment/undo, scenarios and narrow layouts. Use `npm run check`, the complete `npm run test:e2e`, and `npm run test:e2e:iphone`.

The supplied nine monthly UVR statements and annual history/projection were inspected locally read-only. Their original files, filenames, personal identifiers and exact balances are not test fixtures. The monthly format adds a protected-employment insurance column in later periods; the parser reads component headings, rather than assuming fixed positions. One source total differs from its components by COP 0.10; it remains an explicit confirmation issue.

`npm run validate:loan -- --projection <private.xlsx> --rate <EA-percent> --remaining <months> --balance-date <date> --subsidy-rate <EA-percent> --subsidy-start <date> --subsidy-end <date>` performs a read-only comparison against the first two nonzero annual native-UVR projection periods. All assumptions must come from the owner; the command never infers missing subsidy dates. It prints only differences, not balances or identifying data. Rounded-rate comparison tolerances are 0.05 UVR per principal installment and 0.10 UVR on the second closing balance. These are approximation checks, distinct from internal four-decimal rounding. The supplied schedule is within those bounds; exact contractual/daily reconciliation remains unverified. Independent bank COP schedules are still needed: synthetic COP checks do not establish bank conformance.

The official provider returned a valid daily series in a local public-data read on 2026-10-21. This validates the source format, not permanent availability. [Official Suameca series](https://suameca.banrep.gov.co/estadisticas-economicas-back/rest/estadisticaEconomicaRestService/consultaInformacionSerieXTipoDato?idSerie=850&tipoDato=0&cantDatos=5), [official UVR methodology](https://www.banrep.gov.co/sites/default/files/SCE_GT_UVR_Guia_metodologica_V3.pdf).

## Next stage: refinancing and changing denomination

Keep this feature documented but inactive. Require an actual dated lender quote, settlement principal, new rate and convention, term, denomination, amortization, insurance, origination/appraisal/legal/switching costs and verified subsidy consequences. Compare keeping the current loan against buying out/refinancing it under the same household cash and inflation assumptions. Report upfront cash, new installment path, payoff date, total net financing cost, break-even and remaining reserve. Do not invent an offer, approval probability or a universal best strategy. A UVR-to-COP conversion is a new quoted contract, not an edit to the original loan's denomination. Vehicle/consumer loans and purchase affordability require their own validated product rules.
